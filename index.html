Decision dimensions
Every recommendation answers: what should the asset become, where should its value be consumed, and when should the action occur.
Supported routes
Re-lease, redeployment, whole-asset resale, internal recovered-part use, external parts / assembly sale, material recovery and responsible exit.
Non-negotiable controls
No unsupported approval: failed policy or technical gates, missing evidence and uncertain eligibility result in restriction or human review.

Decision memoryPersist evidence snapshot, policy version, model version and approval context.
Exception managementExpired offers, failed tests, cancelled demand and stale evidence trigger re-evaluation.
